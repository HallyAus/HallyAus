## Apps

### 🌱 Plant Planner
A vegetable garden planner built for Australian growing conditions. Plan beds, choose crops, follow seasonal planting guidance and keep garden jobs, harvests and seeds organised.

[Website](https://plantplanner.com.au/) · [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/plant-planner/id6793901685) · [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=au.com.plantplanner)

### 🏡 Widget Studio: Home Assistant
Personal Home Assistant widgets for energy, bins, calendars, lists and more. Choose your sources and layouts, with saved readings available when the connection drops.

[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/widget-studio-home-assistant/id6821856563) · [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.danielhall.widgetstudio) · [Support & setup](https://hallyaus.github.io/widget-studio-support/)

### 🐜 Fire Ant ID
An independent iPhone guide for recording suspected fire ant sightings and finding official Australian reporting services. Built around optional photos, local records and user-controlled sharing. It supports expert assessment rather than automatically identifying a species.

*In development. Public App Store availability not yet confirmed.*

### 🛠️ Made 4 Tradies
Practical tools for Australian tradies: business and website checks, Google review QR codes, editable review replies and job-photo captions.

[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/made-4-tradies/id6823405762)
